I loved this book and its introduction of the rag-tag group of characters, especially their leader (?) Jackson Lamb—I have never seen an agent written with this subtle, sarcastic comedic angle. The book is well paced, and the characters are well motivated. This book got me back into the habit of reading! I feel that over time my opinions and tastes may change; we will see. Honestly, the only reason I started reading this was because of the Apple TV series—they built a very gripping show, and I ended up binge-watching it.
