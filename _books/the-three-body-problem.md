HOLY AM I INVESTED IN THIS SERIES WOW. It is a slow book, but the build-up was worth it. The characters feel a bit mechanical to me. The way Cixin Liu explains the science and technology in depth—I am in awe, and I love it. I wish sci-fi books did this more often, although I have not read enough sci-fi to make that statement with real confidence. I will write a longer review soon, but it will contain spoilers.
