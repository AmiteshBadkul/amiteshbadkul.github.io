It is the second book in the *Slough House* series. Similar to the first one, I had watched the second season of the Apple TV+ series before reading. Good expansion of the spy world and characters into undercover assignments and navigating complex situations. I liked it better than the first one: there were many more situations, both micro and macro, that highlighted the personality and character of the slow horses.
